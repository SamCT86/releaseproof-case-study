# Public disclosure boundary

This repository is a **public engineering case study**, not an open-source release and not a mirror of the private product repository.

## Intentionally public

- the problem being addressed;
- the product/system boundary at a level useful for technical review;
- selected engineering principles and trade-offs;
- defensible technology context;
- the AI-assisted workflow at a non-sensitive level;
- verification philosophy and failure-state handling;
- current status and explicit non-claims;
- public portfolio links.

## Intentionally private

- application source code;
- internal prompts or agent instructions;
- exact schemas, contracts, selectors and verifier implementations;
- private repository paths, commit hashes and internal document names;
- credentials, secrets and environment configuration;
- raw release artifacts, private fixtures and detailed evidence formats;
- unpublished APIs or internal endpoints;
- commercial experiments and private roadmap sequencing;
- implementation details that would materially reproduce the system.

## Why

A hiring manager should be able to inspect **how I frame the problem, where I draw system boundaries, what I make explicit and how I verify behavior** without receiving the proprietary implementation.

Public does not mean open source. No license to the private implementation is granted or implied by this case study.
